# Institutional Layer

The institutional layer serves treasuries, payment operators, custodians, settlement agents and, where appropriately licensed, banking entities.

Capabilities: multi-asset double-entry accounting, sub-ledgers, reserve/liability reconciliation, payment and settlement instructions, escrow, redemption workflows, audit trails, governance approvals, risk limits and incident response.

Separate protocol governance, validation, treasury, custody, reserve attestation, risk, compliance and customer support. No single operational key should combine all roles.

A ledger can prove reconciliation of recorded values; it cannot prove external cash or securities exist. Reserve evidence needs source identity, scope, timestamp, freshness, audit status and limitations.

A software protocol is not a bank charter, payment license, deposit insurance or legal entity. Customer-facing products require appropriately authorized entities and clear debtor, custodian, asset ownership, redemption and insolvency terms.

Minimize personal data on public ledgers. Use opaque identifiers and privacy-preserving commitments where appropriate.
