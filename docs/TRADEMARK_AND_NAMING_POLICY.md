# Naming and conformance policy — proposal

**Status:** proposal, to apply together with [`LICENSE_SPLIT_PROPOSAL.md`](LICENSE_SPLIT_PROPOSAL.md).
It refines section 5 of [`LICENSING_AND_IP_POLICY.md`](LICENSING_AND_IP_POLICY.md). The names
below are used by the project; no trademark registration is claimed or implied, and registration
would need separate counsel. This is not legal advice.

## Why

Apache-2.0 gives no right to use the project's names or logos (its Section 6). The code may be
copied; the right to say "this is SIRM", "official" or "approved by SIRM" is not part of that.

## Rules

1. **Official** is reserved. Only releases published from this project's own repositories under
   its own release process may be called official SIRM or RFT releases.
2. **Describing compatibility is allowed** when true: "implements the SIRM specification
   version X" or "passes the SIRM conformance vectors version X". The claim must name the
   version and the vectors actually run, and must be reproducible by anyone.
3. **No implied endorsement.** A fork, product or service must not suggest it is sponsored,
   reviewed, certified or approved by the project. There is no certification program today;
   nobody may claim "SIRM certified".
4. **Forks and derived products** use their own name and logo, and state clearly that they are
   not the original project.
5. **Networks.** A network or token that is not operated under the project's published
   governance must not use the RFT Network name or present itself as the same network.
6. **Truthful references remain allowed** (naming the project to describe where code or the
   specification came from, quoting it, linking to it).

## Not decided

Whether to register any name or logo; jurisdictions; whether a conformance mark will ever exist;
who may grant naming permission after the owner. These need the owner's decision and counsel.
