# Governance and Control Model

Separate proposal, technical review, approval, execution and audit. Roles include protocol maintainers, formal/implementation reviewers, governance signers, validators, treasury operators, custodians and independent observers.

Protected actions include genesis configuration, supply rules, mint/burn authority, validator-set changes, fees, reserves, upgrades and emergency recovery.

The target is threshold approval over versioned proposals with a timelock for ordinary changes. Emergency pause may be faster but narrowly scoped; unpause and permanent changes require stronger approval.

No founder, administrator, validator or planner may bypass the formally specified issuance transition. The one-time 1.309% creator allocation grants no perpetual administration right, mint authority or claim over treasury assets.

Future Lean work will model roles, signer sets, thresholds, timelocks and protected state, proving unauthorized proposals cannot change it.
